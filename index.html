How Combat Works

Attack vs Defense Rating — On each attack, the attacker rolls 0–ATK and the defender rolls 0–DEF. If the attacker's roll is higher, the attack hits.

Damage vs Armor — Damage dealt is max(1, Damage − Armor + random(−2, 2)). Critical hits bypass armor entirely.

Critical Hits — Deal bonus damage and ignore armor. Stealth attacks always crit.

Stun — When a hero reaches 0 HP they are stunned for 20 ticks, then recover with 20% HP. Stunned heroes can't act and aren't targeted.

Stealth — Stealthed heroes are not targeted by monsters. The next attack from stealth is guaranteed to hit and crit.

Ticks — The game runs at 4 ticks/second. Each character acts every N ticks (their speed).

Regen — HP and SP regenerate a small amount every 3 ticks.

Dungeons — Each dungeon has multiple rooms. The final room contains a boss. Clear all rooms to claim the dungeon.

Castles — After clearing a dungeon, purchase its castle with gold. Castle owners can activate a farm for automatic kill income.

Adventure Points — Earned from achievements and dungeon clears. Bragging rights.

Winning — Clear all 35 dungeons and conquer all castles to end the eternal winter.
