How Combat Works

Attack vs Defense — On each attack, the attacker rolls 0–ATK and defender rolls 0–DEF. Attacker must strictly beat the defender roll to hit.

Damage vs Armor — Damage dealt is max(1, Damage − Armor + random(−2, 2)). Critical hits bypass armor entirely.

Crits & Stealth — Crits deal bonus damage ignoring armor. Stealth attacks always crit with double damage.

Stun — Heroes at 0 HP are stunned for 20 ticks, then recover at 20% HP. They can't act or be targeted while stunned.

Skill Trees — Every hero has a unique 17-node skill tree. Earn 1 skill point per level-up. Allocate nodes to permanently boost stats. Paths branch left, centre, and right — with two keystones and a grand keystone requiring all three paths.

Regen — HP and SP regenerate a small amount every 3 ticks (0.75s).

Dungeons — Each has multiple rooms. The last room holds a boss. Clear all to claim the dungeon.

Castles & Farms — Buy a castle after clearing a dungeon. Activate a farm for passive kill & gold income, including offline.

Winning — Clear all 35 dungeons to conquer the infinite darkness.
