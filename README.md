## Dungeon Infinitum

Dungeon Infinitum is a single‑player idle dungeon crawler that combines automatic real‑time exploration with deep progression systems. Control a party of heroes as they fight through procedurally generated rooms, collect loot, unlock skills, and conquer ever‑stronger dungeons.

# Features

Real‑time Dungeon Crawling – Your party automatically moves, attacks enemies, and collects loot. Use scrolls (keys 1‑6) for instant damage, and activate potions for temporary buffs.

Multiple Character Classes – Choose from eight classes (Fighter, Barbarian, Rogue, Priest, Druid, Electromancer, Pyromancer, Archer), each with unique stats and growth.

Node‑based Progression – Defeat castle bosses to unlock new regions. Each region contains multiple dungeons of increasing difficulty.

Farming System – After clearing a dungeon, you can purchase and activate farms that generate passive gold, XP, and kills over time.

Equipment & Inventory – Collect weapons, armor, and accessories with varying rarities (Common to Legendary). Automatically equip better items.

Scrolls & Potions – Found as loot, scrolls deal damage to all enemies in the current room; potions grant temporary effects like double gold or invincibility.

Skill Trees – Each character can spend skill points to unlock nodes that boost stats and grant special abilities (partial implementation, expandable).

Prestige & Achievements – Earn Adventure Points by killing monsters and clearing dungeons. Spend them on permanent upgrades (extra party slots, faster farms, etc.) and unlock achievements for additional points.

Offline Progress – While away, your farms continue to generate resources (capped at 12 hours).

Save / Load – Automatic local storage saving, plus manual export/import for backups.

# How to Play

Start – The game begins with a Fighter in your party. Use the left panel to select the first dungeon (Goblin Territory) and click "Enter Dungeon".

Combat – Your party automatically moves toward and attacks enemies. Health bars are displayed above each character and monster.

Loot – Defeated monsters may drop gold, items, scrolls, or potions. Your party will automatically pick them up when nearby.

Scrolls & Potions – Press keys 1‑6 to use scrolls from your inventory. Potions can be activated from the Pack tab.

Progression – After clearing a dungeon, new regions unlock. Purchase farms from cleared dungeons to generate passive income.

Party Management – In the Party tab you can add new characters (up to 4 + prestige upgrades) and view their stats. Select a character to see details.

Equipment – The Equipment tab shows equipped items and inventory. Items are auto‑equipped if better than the current one.

Prestige – Once you have earned enough Adventure Points, you can reset your progress in the Prestige tab to gain permanent upgrades.
