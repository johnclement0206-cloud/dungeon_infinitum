## Dungeon Infinitum

Dungeon Infinitum is a single‑player idle text-based dungeon crawler that combines automatic real‑time exploration with deep progression systems. Control a party of heroes as they fight through procedurally generated rooms, collect loot, unlock skills, and conquer ever‑stronger dungeons.

# Features

Real‑time Dungeon Crawling – Your party automatically moves, attacks enemies, and collects loot. Use scrolls (keys 1‑6) for instant damage, and activate potions for temporary buffs.

Multiple Character Classes – Choose from eight classes (Fighter, Barbarian, Rogue, Priest, Druid, Electromancer, Pyromancer, Archer, Chicken King), each with unique stats and growth.

Node‑based Progression – Defeat castle bosses to unlock new regions. Each region contains multiple dungeons of increasing difficulty. **(PARTIALLY IMPLEMENTED)**

Farming System – After clearing a dungeon, you can purchase and activate farms that generate passive gold, XP, and kills over time.

Equipment & Inventory – Collect weapons, armor, and accessories with varying rarities (Common to Legendary). Automatically equip better items.

Scrolls & Potions – Found as loot, scrolls deal damage to all enemies in the current room; potions grant temporary effects like double gold or invincibility. **(CURRENTLY NOT IMPLEMENTED)**

Skill Trees – Each character can spend skill points to unlock nodes that boost stats and grant special abilities.

Prestige & Achievements – Earn Adventure Points by killing monsters and clearing dungeons. Spend them on permanent upgrades (extra party slots, faster farms, etc.) and unlock achievements for additional points. **(PARTIALLY IMPLEMENTED)**

Offline Progress – While away, your farms continue to generate resources (capped at 12 hours).

Save / Load – Automatic local storage saving, plus manual export/import for backups.
